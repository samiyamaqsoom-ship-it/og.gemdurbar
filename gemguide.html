GUIDANCE

Ruby

  • Symbolizes passion, luxury, and confidence.
  • Best cleaned with warm water and soft cloth.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals and direct impact.
  • Popular in rings and royal jewellery designs.
  • Store separately to avoid scratches.

Emerald

  • Represents peace, wisdom, and prosperity.
  • Protect from sudden temperature changes.
  • Use microfiber cloth for safe cleaning.
  • Do not use ultrasonic cleaners.
  • Keep in a soft jewellery pouch.

Sapphire

  • Known for loyalty, strength, and elegance.
  • Highly durable for everyday wear.
  • Clean using mild soap and warm water.
  • Avoid hard impacts to maintain shine.
  • Looks premium in modern jewellery.

Diamond

  • Represents purity and everlasting love.
  • Requires regular cleaning for brilliance.
  • Store carefully to protect other gems.
  • Use soft brush for deep cleaning.
  • Perfect for luxury and engagement jewellery.

Opal

  • Known for creativity and emotional balance.
  • Protect from excessive heat and dryness.
  • Handle carefully because it is delicate.
  • Clean only with soft damp cloth.
  • Store in cool and slightly humid place.

Topaz

  • Associated with clarity and confidence.
  • Avoid long exposure to sunlight.
  • Wash gently with lukewarm water.
  • Protect from scratches and hard falls.
  • Excellent choice for elegant jewellery.

Amethyst

  • Represents calmness and spiritual balance.
  • Keep away from prolonged sunlight.
  • Clean using soft cloth and mild soap.
  • Popular for stylish fashion jewellery.
  • Store safely to preserve color quality.

Aquamarine

  • Known for serenity and clarity.
  • Clean gently using warm water.
  • Avoid rough handling and hard surfaces.
  • Store in separate soft-lined box.
  • Popular for elegant blue jewellery designs.
